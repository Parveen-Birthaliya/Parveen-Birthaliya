Exercise 2: Marketing Message Evaluation
Audience: Business owners or managers who want more work done.

Missing info: How it improves? By how much percent? Is it saving time or money?

Convincing?: No, too boring and everyone say this.

Better version: "Our ERP automates daily data entry to save your team 10 hours every week."

Exercise 3: Feature-to-Benefit Thinking
Meaning: "Cloud-based" means software runs on internet, not on your office computer hard disk.

2 Benefits:

You can check data from anywhere using phone.

No need to buy expensive servers for office.

Why useful: It save money and let team work from home easily.

Exercise 4: Decision Making
Excel limits: Too many manual typing, easy to make mistake, slow when data is big.

ERP advantages: All data in one place, automatic reports, no double entry.

Comparison: Excel is free but waste time when startup grows. ERP cost money but helps grow fast without mistakes.

Decision: Startups should use ERP if they have budget, else start Excel but plan to move soon.

Exercise 5: Problem Analysis
Root cause: People typing data by hand into system.

How delays happen: Human type slow, make typos, then others have to find mistake and fix it.

Solution: Use automatic software or scanner to import data directly without typing.

ReAct (REASON + ACT) PROMPTING
Exercise 1: Weak Statement Improvement
Critique: "Good" means nothing. It don't tell what the software actually do or why customer should care.

Missing: Specific features, results, or value.

Better: "Our retail software tracks your inventory automatically so you never run out of stock."

Exercise 2: Resume Enhancement
Weakness: "Worked on testing" is weak. It don't show what tools you use or what you achieved.

Add info: Used Selenium tool, found 50+ bugs, made software stable.

Better: "Led automated testing using Selenium, reducing software bugs by 30% before launch."

Exercise 3: CTA Optimization
Critique: "Click here" is old and don't give reason to click.

Add benefit: Tell them what they get if they click.

Better: "Get Your Free Trial Now"

Exercise 4: Email Content Fix
Critique: Very dry sentence. Don't show how it helps the reader.

Target: Small business owner who is stressed.

Better: "We offer ERP solutions that handle your billing automatically so you can focus on sales."

Exercise 5: Bug Description Rewrite
Missing: When it is slow? Which page? What button you clicked?

Details needed: Page name, steps to reproduce, loading time.

Better: "Checkout page takes 15 seconds to load after clicking 'Confirm Payment' button on Chrome browser."

SELF-CONSISTENCY PROMPTING
Exercise 1: Short Description Refinement
Version 1: ERP is software for business. It manages your stocks, finance, and HR in one system.

Review: A bit dry. Need to show business value.

Refined: ERP is a central software that connects your sales, inventory, and finance to save time and stop mistakes.

Exercise 2: FAQ Answer Quality
3 Answers:

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning and manages business.

ERP is a software that connects all your department data together.

ERP automates your office work like billing and inventory tracking.

Best one & Reason: Answer 2 and 3 mixed is best because it explains what it is and what it does simply.

Refined Best Answer: ERP is a central software that connects all your departments—like sales and inventory—so your business runs automatically in one place.

Exercise 3: Email Subject Lines
2 Variations:

Invitation to ERP software demo.

Save 5 hours a week: See our ERP demo.

Evaluation: Version 2 is much better because it shows a clear benefit immediately.

Refined Subject: "Free 15-Min Demo: How to save 5 hours every week with ERP"

Exercise 4: Technical Explanation Simplification
Original: Cloud computing is the on-demand availability of computer system resources like data storage and computing power over the internet.

Review: Too textbook style. "On-demand availability" is hard word.

Simplified: Cloud computing means storing and running your software on the internet instead of your own computer's hard drive.

Exercise 5: Role-Based Messaging
Version 1: ERP helps manager see reports and track what employees are doing every day.

Review: Sounds like spying. Change tone to show how it helps manager make good decisions easily.

Refined: ERP gives managers real-time dashboard reports so you can make fast decisions without asking staff for updates constantly.
